## Ingredients

- potatoes
- parsley
- bacon
- 2 tablespoons butter
- black pepper
- cheese
- sour cream
- chives

## Instructions

1. Air fry potatoes for about 20 minutes.
2. Scoop out potatoes and add sour cream.
3. Mix in parsley, bacon, butter, black pepper, and cheese.
4. Add the mix back into the potatoes.
5. Return to the air fryer with cheese on top for 5 minutes at 400.
6. Top with bacon, chopped chives, and sour cream.
