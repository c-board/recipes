## Ingredients

- chicken breasts
- salt
- black pepper
- cooked white rice
- chili crisp
- capers
- green onions
- garlic powder (optional)
- olive oil
- butter
- flour
- chicken broth

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken breasts with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder if using.
2. Cook the chicken in a skillet over medium heat for 5–7 minutes per side until cooked through.
3. Rest the chicken for 3–5 minutes, then slice.
4. In the same pan, warm a splash of olive oil with butter over medium heat.
5. Whisk in a touch of flour and cook for 1 minute.
6. Slowly whisk in chicken broth and simmer for 2–3 minutes until lightly thickened into a white sauce.
7. Warm the rice and spoon it into bowls, then ladle a little white sauce around the rice.
8. Top with sliced chicken, spoon chili crisp over the chicken, and scatter capers and sliced green onions on top.
9. Serve immediately.
