## Ingredients

- chicken breasts or thighs
- olive oil
- salt
- black pepper
- garlic powder
- paprika
- baby potatoes
- broccoli
- skewers

## Instructions

1. Cut chicken into bite-sized chunks and toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
2. Thread the chicken onto skewers.
3. Grill over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, for 8–12 minutes until charred and cooked through.
4. Slice slits in the potatoes and smoke them for 45–60 minutes.
5. Steam the broccoli for 5 minutes.
6. Serve the skewers hot with the potatoes and broccoli.
