## Ingredients

- center round cut of beef
- 2 tablespoons black pepper
- 1 smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cajun
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon honey

## Instructions

1. Marinade overnight.
2. Smoke on Traeger for 6–8 hours to taste.
