## Ingredients

- ground beef
- croissant
- habanero cheese
- lettuce
- japanese mayo
- sriracha
- fresh white onion

## Instructions

1. Form the ground beef into patties and season both sides. Cook in a skillet or on the grill for 3–4 minutes per side until done to your liking.
2. Cut the burger in half horizontally after cooking and put the cheese in the middle.
3. Slice the croissants in half and toast with the cheese and burger together for 1–2 minutes.
4. Build the burger: bottom croissant, patty with cheese, japanese mayo and sriracha, sliced white onion, lettuce, top croissant. Serve immediately.
