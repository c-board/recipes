## Ingredients

- ribs
- seasoning
- light brown sugar
- honey
- butter

## Instructions

1. Cut ends so it's flat on the ends.
2. Season overnight.
3. Cook at 275°F for 2.5 hours.
4. Bring in and season again.
5. Lay down tin foil with light brown sugar, honey, and butter.
6. Wrap up meat side down.
7. Cook for another 2 hours at 275°F.
