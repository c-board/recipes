## Ingredients

### Sushi Rice

- 1 cup rice
- 1.75 cups water

### Brown Rice

- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water

### Mixed Rice

- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water

## Instructions

1. Rinse rice.
2. For sushi rice, cook with the listed water ratio for 18 minutes.
3. For brown or mixed rice, cook with the listed water ratio for 40–45 minutes.
