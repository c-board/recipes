## Ingredients

- 1 slice of bread
- 1 egg
- butter
- salt
- black pepper
- fresh chives
- hot sauce
- pickled onions
- pickled jalapenos

## Instructions

1. Cut a hole in the center of the bread slice.
2. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Place the bread in the pan and crack the egg into the hole.
4. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the bottom is golden, then flip and cook for 1–2 minutes to desired doneness.
5. Season with salt and pepper and sprinkle with chopped chives.
6. Drizzle with hot sauce and serve with pickled onions and jalapenos on the side.
